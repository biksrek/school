ABOUT US

Welcome to Batase Brindavan.

Located in the beautiful rural community of Maijogmai Rural Municipality–4, Ilam, Nepal, Batase Brindavan English Boarding School is committed to providing quality and affordable education to children from Playgroup to Grade 8.

With over 120 students, our mission is to ensure that children from rural areas receive the same educational opportunities as those in towns and cities. We firmly believe that every child deserves access to quality education, regardless of their financial background or location.

OUR STORY

Founded in 2067 BS for Rural Empowerment.

OUR FOUNDERS

Mrs. Anjana Gurung & Mr. Pasang Yonzon

Founded by Mrs. Anjana Gurung and Mr. Pasang Yonzon in the year 2067 BS, Batase Brindavan English Boarding School was established with a vision of empowering rural students through academic excellence, discipline, creativity, and strong moral values.

Our founders recognized the educational gap between urban centers and rural communities. They established the school in Maijogmai–4 to ensure children in rural Ilam receive top-tier, affordable education.

At our school, we offer scholarships to support students from low-income backgrounds, ensuring that they have access to education despite financial challenges.
